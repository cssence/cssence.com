Farewell Twitter

The final chapter.

Editorial
2026-07-08T09:40:00Z

Next week would have been be my fifteenth Twitter anniversary, but that ain’t gonna happen, and it’s anyhow not a thing.

For two reasons: Someone renamed Twitter, but more importantly, I deactivated my account 30 days ago, meaning the grace period is over and the account is gone. No big deal, I replaced it with Mastodon in 2022 and after an unnecessary course correction I stopped tweeting in 2024. But the account still existed.

For two reasons: To avoid link rot, and to prevent others from grabbing the account. So why now? What changed?

I stopped caring. But in general it’s not a good idea to have any kind of association with the platform. Even when you think it had reached rock bottom, it manages to take a turn for the worse. Good riddance.

Better times

Before turning off the lights, I picked a handful of tweets so I could create an image gallery. I obviously did this way too late, conversations with people that themselves had deleted their accounts have already been broken. And even though I have all the data required to restore these on my own site, I couldn’t be arsed to spend the time to create mockups just so I could take screenshots.

But I did sneak something in. For older tweets that predate a special 💒 day in my life, I made sure that my birth name was shown in the screenshot. After all, I did use the platform for more then a decade, and a lot has happened during that time.

I suppose the gallery is my personal acknowledgment that Twitter was the place to be back then.

Okay, fine. I’m not really into nostalgia. I just wanted an excuse to do something with masonry layout, and this is problably my one and only chance. Goes without saying, support for grid lanes ain’t there yet, so I did the next best thing, or several things, depending on the site’s page style.

  • On an unstyled page, the images are inline elements, as one would expect.
  • In the Basic page style, I used floats. 😬
  • In the Advanced page style, I called flexbox to the rescue.
  • For the time being, the Elegant page style is almost an exact copy of Advanced, but I’ll replace it with actual grid lanes when browser support finally gets there.
